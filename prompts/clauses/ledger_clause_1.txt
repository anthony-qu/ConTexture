Some execute_python results carry an extra block headed `[STATE]`. You did not print it —
  it is written for you by inspecting the AnnData and MuData objects in your session, and it
  appears only when something about them changed, for example from the previous analysis step. 
  It reports facts and never a verdict: for each object the shape and the dtype, storage and 
  value domain of X; for an obs or var column, the distribution of that whole column.

  Read those lines very carefully as they show you a record of analysis performed on this object.
  They are also often where a mistake first becomes visible. A number in a `[STATE]` block is
  a check you ran, so the gate rule below applies to it: say in one line what it implies, and if it
  says the result is implausible, fix it before going on.
